Awareness

Types of QR Code Scams

Understanding common QR code fraud techniques helps you stay protected.

💳

Fake Payment QR Codes

Scammers place fake QR codes over legitimate ones at shops, parking meters, and restaurants, redirecting payments to their accounts.

High Risk
🎣

Phishing URL QR Codes

QR codes that redirect to fake websites mimicking banks or payment apps to steal login credentials and OTPs.

High Risk
🎁

Lottery / Prize Scams

QR codes promising cash prizes, lottery winnings, or rewards that require "small payments" to claim.

Medium Risk
🔄

Reverse Payment Scams

Fraudsters send collect requests disguised as payment receipts, tricking users into approving money transfers.

High Risk
📧

KYC Update Scams

QR codes claiming to be from banks requiring KYC verification, leading to phishing sites that capture personal information.

High Risk
🏪

Tampered Merchant QR

Physical QR stickers at shops replaced with fraudulent ones. The original merchant's QR is covered with a scammer's QR code.

High Risk

Safety Guide

Prevention Tips

Follow these best practices to protect yourself from QR code fraud.

1

Verify Before Scanning

Always check if a QR code sticker has been tampered with or placed over another one. Look for signs of overlay or tampering.

2

Check the URL

After scanning, verify the URL or UPI ID before proceeding. Look for misspellings, suspicious domains, and non-HTTPS links.

3

Never Share OTP

No legitimate payment system will ask for your OTP through a QR code. If a page asks for OTP after scanning, it's a scam.

4

Use Trusted Apps

Only scan QR codes using official banking or payment apps. Avoid third-party QR scanner apps that may be malicious.

5

Verify Payment Direction

Ensure you are paying, not receiving a collect request. Scammers send collect requests disguised as payment confirmations.

6

Report Suspicious QR Codes

If you encounter a suspicious QR code, report it to Cyber Crime Helpline 1930 or use our platform to analyze and report.

About The Project

QR Code Scam Prevention System

This project was developed as part of a college assignment to address the growing problem of QR code fraud in India's digital payment ecosystem. With mobile payments becoming the norm, malicious QR codes have become a serious threat.

Our system uses rule-based fraud detection algorithms — not AI — to analyze QR code contents and identify potential scams. It checks UPI payment details, URLs, and patterns against a database of known threats.
